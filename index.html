Week 10 Prototype Notes

This prototype demonstrates the proposed Customer Information & Relationship Tool for Cơm Nhà Restaurant. It is intentionally limited to the agreed project scope and does not replace the restaurant's full POS or reservation platform.

Purpose Help staff identify new and returning customers and access useful relationship information.
Current implementation Browser-based prototype using sample data. Jimmy's feedback has been incorporated into the current design.
Next steps Test the revised workflow, confirm the final new/returning rule, and document client feedback and outcomes.
Out of scope Full POS replacement, live financial processing, payment card storage, bulk marketing automation and website redesign.
